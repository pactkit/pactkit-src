# Spec BUG-005: board.py archive vs classify inconsistent for taskless stories
- **Release**: 1.1.2

## Context
`archive_stories()` archives any story block without `- [ ]` (unchecked tasks), including stories with **zero tasks**. But `_classify_story()` classifies zero-done-count stories as `backlog` even when todo count is also zero. This means:
- `fix_board()` puts a taskless story in Backlog (via `_classify_story`)
- `archive_stories()` treats a taskless story as Done (moves to archive)

## Requirements
- The `archive_stories()` function MUST NOT archive stories that have zero tasks (no `- [x]` and no `- [ ]`).
- The `_classify_story()` classification MUST remain the source of truth for story status.
- The system SHOULD keep taskless stories in Backlog until tasks are added.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario 1: Taskless story not archived
- **Given** a board with a story that has no task checkboxes
- **When** `archive_stories()` is called
- **Then** the taskless story remains on the board and is not archived

### Scenario 2: Story with all tasks done is still archived
- **Given** a board with a story where all tasks are `[x]`
- **When** `archive_stories()` is called
- **Then** the story is archived (existing behavior preserved)

### Scenario 3: Story with unchecked tasks not archived
- **Given** a board with a story that has unchecked `- [ ]` tasks
- **When** `archive_stories()` is called
- **Then** the story is not archived (existing behavior preserved)

## Design
Add a guard in `archive_stories()` to check that the story block contains at least one `- [x]` before treating it as archivable. This aligns archive logic with `_classify_story()`.

## Target Call Chain
`archive_stories()` → `re.split()` → archive condition check
